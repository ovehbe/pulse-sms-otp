🔑 Pulse SMS OTP Helper

Automatically detect and copy OTP codes from your messages

⚡ Auto-Copy OTP Codes

When enabled, OTP codes will be automatically copied to your clipboard as soon as they're detected in your messages. You'll see a confirmation on the copy button.

ℹ️ How It Works

On the Pulse SMS conversations page:

  • The extension scans message previews for OTP codes (4-8 digits)
  • A blue "Copy OTP" button appears in the page header
  • Click the button to copy the detected code anytime
  • With auto-copy enabled, codes are copied immediately when detected

Tip: You don't need to open individual conversations — the extension reads codes directly from the conversation list!
